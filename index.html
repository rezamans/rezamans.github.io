About

Building enterprise IT that runs itself

Most IT environments are full of repetitive manual work. I find the patterns, automate them away, and leave teams with reliable systems they can actually scale.

I'm an IT Infrastructure & Automation Engineer based in Vancouver with over fifteen years designing, deploying, and maintaining enterprise IT environments. My focus is building scalable systems that reduce manual work, improve operational consistency, and let IT teams manage infrastructure more efficiently.

I work fluently across Windows, macOS, and Linux (Fedora, CentOS, Ubuntu) and have hands-on production experience with enterprise networking and security — Cisco (CCNP/CCNA), MikroTik, Sophos, and Fortinet — including deep LAN troubleshooting. Most recently I've been leading IT operations at a multi-clinic healthcare network, turning 45-minute manual setups into single-command workflows with PowerShell, Jamf Pro, and modern deployment frameworks.

Beyond the technical work, I lead and grow IT teams. I've personally trained over 25 engineers, and I'm currently driving a standardization program at Terra Nova — turning ad-hoc operations into documented, repeatable, scalable processes. I believe the strongest IT leaders multiply themselves through clear documentation, knowledge transfer, and consistent standards.

The work I'm proudest of bridges technical capability and real-world usability — systems that don't just function, but that other people can use, maintain, and trust.
